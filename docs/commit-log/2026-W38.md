## Thursday — 2026-09-17

### Service Center Auto-Match Accuracy Fixes

Commit: `f2652e8`

`fix(cms): exclude inactive Service Centers and collapse duplicate area rows from auto-match`

Adds an `active !== false` guard to both `buildDistrictPolygonIndex` and
`resolveServiceCenterMatch` in `serviceCenterMatch.ts` so a retired district
or Service Center can never be auto-matched, and de-duplicates candidate
Area rows by `id` before judging ambiguity so a duplicate row (same Service
Center, stale label) no longer masks an otherwise-unambiguous match. Also
rewrites `stubResolveServiceCenter` in `serviceCenterResolve.ts` to always
reject instead of guessing the first candidate, since `useResolveServiceCenter`'s
existing `.catch` already leaves the field untouched on rejection. New tests
added to `serviceCenterMatch.test.ts` and a new `serviceCenterResolve.test.ts`.
